## Overview

From **October 2024 to May 2026**, my tenure at CGI Philippines evolved across three progressive scopes on an enterprise banking engagement: starting as a **Service Desk Specialist**, expanding into **Knowledge Management** and client-facing **Problem Management**, and earning promotion into **Service Integration and Management (SIAM)**.

## Phase 02 — Service Integration and Management (Promoted)

Following the impact of my knowledge management and service desk optimization initiatives, I moved into a **SIAM** role coordinating across multiple internal and client departments.

- Worked directly across departments to bridge operational handoff gaps and drive measurable service improvements across the engagement.
- Connected frontline Service Desk insights with broader service governance, problem elimination, and delivery quality standards.

## Phase 01 — Service Desk Specialist & Knowledge Manager

Beginning on the frontline supporting bank employees with day-to-day enterprise incidents, deep product knowledge created the opportunity to work directly with clients beyond reactive ticket handling.

- **Client-Facing Problem Management**: Collaborated directly with client teams to identify troubleshooting improvements for known issues and raise structured **problem tasks** aimed at eliminating incident recurrence at the root.
- **Knowledge Base Modernization (~66% Overhaul)**: Took ownership as Knowledge Manager—updating nearly two-thirds of the entire Service Desk knowledge library from a legacy style book into modern, concise, step-by-step guides built for both end-users and Service Desk analysts.
- **Access Control Friction Reduction**: Led initiatives to streamline repetitive daily access-control tasks that previously required extensive manual rework, freeing Service Desk analysts to focus on higher-impact incidents.
- **Enterprise Microsoft Operations**: Built deep hands-on operational fluency across **Microsoft 365**, **Microsoft Intune**, **Active Directory**, **Endpoint & Device Management**, **SCCM**, **Microsoft Entra**, **Azure**, and **Privileged Identity Management (PIM)**.

## Key Outcomes

- **75%–85% reduction in troubleshooting time** for recurring incidents—cutting resolution duration on targeted known issues from **~20 minutes down to 3–5 minutes**.
- **~66% of the Service Desk knowledge base** modernized from legacy reference material into concise, standardized operational guidance.
- **Reduced daily operational friction** across repetitive access-control workflows, increasing analyst capacity for priority incidents.
- **Promoted internally** into Service Integration and Management (SIAM).
