## Overview

As a **Service Improvement Analyst** within IT Service Management at **Sun Life Global Solutions** (Taguig, PH), I bridge enterprise service governance with hands-on software engineering—turning fragmented operational exports into clear, standardized visibility for service owners and leadership.

## Flagship Initiative — SunScore

I am designing and building **SunScore**, an internal Service Management Scoring Engine built with **React / TypeScript**, a **Python** scoring engine, **PostgreSQL**, **Microsoft Entra** authentication, and **AWS EC2** hosting.

- **Service Reliability Index (SRI)**: Normalizes configured columns extracted from **ServiceNow** (`.csv` / `.xlsx` exports today, targeting live ServiceNow integration) and applies component-level weights to calibrate four core domains—**Incident, Change, Problem, and Service Availability**—into a single unified score.
- **Clarity on Service Health**: Eliminates the recurring operational question of *"What service is working and what isn't?"* by surfacing which services require immediate attention and where deeper stability measures are needed to safeguard delivery quality.
- **Application Risk Register**: Introduces a dedicated second ingestion layer tracking **Technical Debt** and **Cyber Exposure** per service or application—pairing operational reliability with risk posture in one consolidated view.

## Key Responsibilities

- Translate multi-domain ITSM practices into weighted, auditable scoring criteria aligned with Continual Service Improvement (CSI) goals.
- Architect ingestion pipelines, normalization schemas, and clear stakeholder views that connect raw ServiceNow operational data directly to remediation priorities.
- Partner with service teams to act on reliability and risk signals before recurring instability impacts business delivery.
