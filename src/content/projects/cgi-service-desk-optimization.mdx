## Context

In an enterprise banking Service Desk environment, operational drag compounds rapidly when analysts rely on outdated legacy documentation, repeatedly troubleshoot known issues without root-cause follow-through, and spend hours every day executing friction-heavy access-control requests.

Starting as a **Service Desk Specialist** at CGI Philippines in **October 2024**, I built deep product and platform fluency across the Microsoft enterprise stack and expanded my scope into **Problem Management** and **Knowledge Management** to lift performance across the entire desk.

## Key Initiatives

### 1. Knowledge Base Overhaul (~66% Modernized)

- Stepping into the role of **Knowledge Manager**, I rebuilt nearly **66% of the entire Service Desk knowledge library**—converting dense, legacy style-book documentation into modern, concise, step-by-step directions.
- Designed articles to serve both **end-users** and **Service Desk analysts**, cutting lookup ambiguity and standardizing troubleshooting across shifts.

### 2. Client-Facing Problem Management & Faster Triage

- Worked directly with client teams to surface troubleshooting bottlenecks on recurring known issues and create structured **problem tasks** focused on eliminating incident recurrence rather than repeatedly patching symptoms.
- By pairing clear root-cause runbooks with streamlined diagnostic paths, troubleshooting time on targeted recurring incidents dropped from **~20 minutes down to 3–5 minutes**—a **75%–85% reduction**.

### 3. Access Control Workflow Simplification

- Identified repetitive daily access-control tasks that required extensive repeated manual effort by analysts.
- Led process improvements to reduce approval and execution friction, reclaiming analyst bandwidth for higher-priority, business-critical banking incidents.

### 4. Enterprise Platform Depth

Executed daily operations and improvement workflows across the Microsoft enterprise ecosystem, including **Microsoft 365**, **Microsoft Intune**, **Active Directory**, **Endpoint & Device Management**, **SCCM**, **Microsoft Entra**, **Azure**, and **Privileged Identity Management (PIM)**.

## Outcome

- **75%–85% faster troubleshooting** on targeted recurring incidents (**20 min → 3–5 min**).
- **~66% of the Service Desk knowledge base** modernized into concise, actionable documentation.
- **Lower daily operational friction** on repetitive access-control requests across the team.
- **Promoted to Service Integration and Management (SIAM)**, working across departments to bridge gaps and deliver project-wide service improvements through **May 2026**.
